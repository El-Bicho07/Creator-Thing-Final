# Golden Evaluation Scenarios — Creator SaaS Platform

*Contains 10 primary golden scenarios used for automated & manual regression evaluation of the integrated Creator SaaS Platform.*

---

## Scenario 1: Tech Educator — System Design Content
- **Niche:** Software Engineering / System Design
- **Target Audience:** Mid-level Developers & Tech Leads
- **Creator Voice Profile:** Direct, concise, technical, uses bullet points, no fluff, short sentences.
- **Input Goal:** Generate a 60-second Reel script explaining API Rate Limiting algorithms (Token Bucket vs Leaky Bucket).
- **Expected Output:**
  - Hook: "Most developers pick the wrong rate limiting algorithm and crash their server."
  - Body: Concise comparison of Token Bucket vs Leaky Bucket with visual B-roll cues.
  - Voice Match: $\ge 85\%$ match with developer tone.
  - CTA: "Comment 'SCALE' for my full backend architecture breakdown."

---

## Scenario 2: Fitness Coach — Daily Content Plan
- **Niche:** Postpartum Fitness & Nutrition
- **Target Audience:** Busy mothers with 30 minutes/day for exercise.
- **Creator Voice Profile:** Encouraging, empathetic, practical, conversational.
- **Input Goal:** Generate today's content plan with a 45-minute total creation budget.
- **Expected Output:**
  - Recommended Plan: 1 Carousel post on 5-minute core exercises + 1 Instagram Reel on quick protein snacks.
  - Fallback Idea: Quick Q&A response Reel based on recent audience comments.
  - Actionability: Time breakdown provided (15 mins script, 20 mins film, 10 mins edit).

## Scenario 3: Productivity Creator — RAG Memory Query
- **Niche:** Personal Development & Notion Workflows
- **Target Audience:** Knowledge workers & college students.
- **User Query to Library:** "Have I created any content about time-blocking before?"
- **Expected Output:**
  - Semantic Search Retrieval: Retrieves 3 past posts/transcripts related to time-blocking.
  - Synthesized Answer: "Yes, you published a YouTube video on '3 Time-Blocking Hacks' 2 months ago and an Instagram Reel on 'Time-Blocking vs Task Lists'. Your top performing angle was the '2-minute buffer rule'."

---

## Scenario 4: Finance Creator — Comment-to-Content Transformation
- **Niche:** Index Fund Investing & Personal Finance
- **Audience Comment:** "Should I invest all my money at once or do Dollar-Cost Averaging?"
- **Input Goal:** Convert comment into a high-engagement Reel script.
- **Expected Output:**
  - Hook: "Lump sum vs Dollar-Cost Averaging: Here is what 100 years of stock market data says."
  - Script Structure: Hook → Data evidence → Simple recommendation → CTA.
  - Tone Alignment: Financial accuracy + simple analogies.

---

## Scenario 5: SaaS Founder — Hook Generation
- **Niche:** Bootstrap SaaS & Indie Hacking
- **Topic:** How we reached $10k MRR without paid ads.
- **Input Goal:** Generate 10 hooks across 5 emotional hook styles.
- **Expected Output:**
  - 10 distinct hooks categorized into Curiosity Gap, Negative Hook, Story Hook, Data-driven, and Question Hook.
  - Zero generic openings.

---

## Scenario 6: Career Coach — Content Repurposing
- **Niche:** Resume & Interview Preparation
- **Original Content:** A 300-word draft on negotiating a tech salary.
- **Input Goal:** Format for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, and YouTube Shorts.
- **Expected Output:**
  - LinkedIn: Professional formatting, line breaks, strong opening line, career hashtag set.
  - X Thread: 5 concise, punchy tweets with thread indicators (1/5).
  - Instagram Caption: Engaging narrative with call-to-action to save post.
  - Shorts Script: 45-second spoken script with scene direction.

---

## Scenario 7: AI Creator — Research Synthesis
- **Niche:** AI Tools & Automation
- **Research Topic:** Local LLMs vs Cloud APIs for Small Business.
- **Input Goal:** Extract facts, controversial angles, myth busting, and source suggestions.
- **Expected Output:**
  - 3 Core Facts (latency, privacy, cost).
  - 2 Controversy Angles ("Cloud APIs are getting too expensive for startups").
  - 1 Myth Busted ("Myth: Local LLMs require $5000 GPUs").

## Scenario 8: Lifestyle Creator — Thumbnail Ideation
- **Niche:** Minimalist Living & Decluttering
- **Video Title:** "I deleted 90% of my belongings — Here's what happened."
- **Input Goal:** Generate 3 visual thumbnail concepts and text overlay ideas.
- **Expected Output:**
  - Concept 1: Split screen before/after room with high-contrast text overlay: "LESS = MORE".
  - Color palette: Warm neutral tones, bold white typography.

---

## Scenario 9: Business Coach — Performance Feedback (Coach)
- **Niche:** Executive Coaching & Leadership
- **Performance Input:** Recent Reel got 500 views (average is 5000 views).
- **Input Goal:** Analyze why performance dropped and generate actionable lesson.
- **Expected Output:**
  - Scorecard: Hook score 2/5 (slow 5-second intro), Pacing score 3/5.
  - Lesson: "Your opening line 'Hi guys today I want to talk about leadership' lost 70% of viewers in the first 3 seconds. Start directly with the problem."

---

## Scenario 10: Growth Creator — CTA Adaptation
- **Niche:** E-commerce & Shopify Growth
- **Goal:** Lead Magnet Download (Free E-commerce Checklist).
- **Input Goal:** Generate 5 soft and 5 direct CTAs.
- **Expected Output:**
  - Soft CTA: "If you're building a store right now, save this for your next launch."
  - Direct CTA: "Comment 'CHECKLIST' below and I'll send you the exact launch roadmap."
